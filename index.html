Privacy Policy for Savora

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Savora respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Savora handles information when you use the Savora mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Savora does not require you to create an account and does not directly request information such as your name, email address, phone number, password, or payment information.

When you use Savora's download functionality, the link you submit may be sent to Savora's backend service so that the requested media can be processed and the resulting file can be delivered to your device.

Please do not submit links or information that you do not have the right to process or download.

2. Downloaded Files

Downloaded files are stored locally on your device so that you can view and manage your downloads within the application.

Savora does not intentionally use your downloaded files for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

3. Advertising

Savora uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process certain device and advertising-related information, such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, approximate location, ad interaction information, and diagnostic information, depending on the advertising configuration and applicable consent choices.

Google may use this information to provide, measure, and improve advertising services and, where permitted, personalize advertisements.

For more information about how Google uses data when you use apps that use its services, please visit Google's privacy information.

4. Third-Party Services

Savora may use third-party services required to provide application functionality, including Google AdMob for advertising and Savora's backend infrastructure for processing download requests.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

5. Data Security

Savora takes reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application and its backend services. Data transmitted between the application and supported services is intended to be protected using secure network connections where supported.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Savora does not require a user account and therefore does not maintain an account profile containing personal information.

Downloaded files can be deleted by the user from their device. Information temporarily processed by the backend to fulfill a download request is not intended to be retained longer than reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality, except where technical, security, legal, or operational requirements require limited retention.

7. Children's Privacy

Savora is not directed specifically toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating user accounts or profiles.

8. Your Choices

You can stop using Savora at any time and remove downloaded files from your device.

Advertising preferences and consent choices may also be available through Google's advertising and consent mechanisms where applicable.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Savora, its services, or applicable requirements. Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or Savora's privacy practices, you may contact the developer through the GitHub repository associated with this Privacy Policy by using the repository's Issues or contact mechanism.

11. Consent

By using Savora, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to the practices described in it, subject to your rights and choices under applicable privacy laws.
